## Overview

Co-launched the Global Clean Energy Innovation Exchange Network (GCxN) at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory in partnership with Shell. The accelerator advanced and de-risked early-stage clean energy technology companies by providing access to NREL's world-class facilities, expertise, and testing infrastructure.

## Key Accomplishments

- **$10.5M+** in funded work directed as PI across advanced vehicle technologies and fuels initiatives
- Designed and operationalized the accelerator model from concept to sustained operation
- Built partnerships between startups, national lab researchers, and corporate sponsors
- Multiple cohorts of cleantech startups successfully advanced through the program

## Legacy

GCxN demonstrated that national laboratories can serve as effective innovation platforms for early-stage companies. The model has influenced how NREL and other DOE labs engage with the startup ecosystem.
