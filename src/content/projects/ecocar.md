## Overview

Leading the Colorado School of Mines team in the U.S. Department of Energy's national Battery Workforce Challenge. Secured EcoCAR Innovation Challenge funding (~$520K over four years) to support student-led research and development in advanced battery and vehicle electrification technologies.

## Key Accomplishments

- **Fourth-place national finish** in BWC Year 2 competition
- **~$520K** in EcoCAR Innovation Challenge funding secured as PI
- Multi-disciplinary team spanning ME, EE, and CS departments
- Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools and processes
- Partnership with Stellantis and DOE national laboratories

## Impact

The program provides students with authentic engineering experience in battery technology, vehicle electrification, and systems integration — areas of critical workforce need. Graduates consistently place at top-tier companies in the automotive and energy sectors.
